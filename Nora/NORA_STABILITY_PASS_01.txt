STABILITY PASS 01 — centralized touch resolver

Changed:
- Added TouchResolver autoload. It is now the single owner of deciding which draggable Area2D receives a touch.
- Removed per-object input_event drag-start race from draggable.gd.
- draggable.gd now exposes can_start_drag() + begin_drag(screen_pos); movement/drop behavior is otherwise kept intact.
- When several draggable objects overlap, portable decor/characters are preferred over furniture; then visual z/order breaks ties deterministically.

What to test on device:
1. Put pillow/decor over sofa/table and grab the decor repeatedly from overlapping areas.
2. Overlap two furniture pieces in Build mode and check that the visually top intended one is selected consistently.
3. Drag character where it overlaps furniture.
4. Confirm ordinary empty-space horizontal camera drag still works.
5. Confirm release/drop, falling, seating and saving still behave as before.

This pass intentionally does NOT change depth sorting, Surface/RestY, footprints, bounds, spring startup or save hierarchy. Those are later stability passes so regressions stay attributable.
