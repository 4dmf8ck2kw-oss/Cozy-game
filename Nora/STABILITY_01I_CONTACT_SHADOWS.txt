01I CONTACT SHADOW SYSTEM
Base: 01H (known working on iPad/Xogot).

Changed only shadow rendering/building:
- Generic shadows no longer derive from full sprite width.
- ItemBuilder scans a shallow band of bottom opaque pixels and creates separate contact lobes for actual artwork contact regions (legs/base).
- Shadow renderer draws three low-alpha static layers per lobe for soft contact edges; no realtime Light2D, shader, timers, or per-frame shadow work.
- Existing scene-authored sofa shadow reduced to a subtle contact strip.
- No touch, drag, placement, surface, save, GameMode, or resource-loading logic changed.

Mobile intent: static CanvasItem drawing, redrawn only when configured; avoids realtime 2D light/shadow cost.
- Old sofa now has two small contact shadows under its actual bottom contact/leg regions instead of one broad oval.
